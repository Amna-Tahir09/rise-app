SOURCE: Al-Ghazali, Kimiya-yi Sa'adat (The Alchemy of Happiness), trans. Claud Field, 1910, public domain
A certain sheikh once had a disciple whom he favoured above his other disciples, thus exciting their envy. One day the sheikh gave each of them a fowl and told each to go and kill it in a place where no one could see him. Each killed his fowl in some retired spot, except the sheikh's favourite disciple, who brought his back alive, saying, "I have found no such place, for Allah sees everywhere." The sheikh said to the others, "You see now this youth's real rank."
