SOURCE: Al-Ghazali, Ihya Ulum al-Din, trans. Syed Nawab Ali, 1921, public domain
When a man feels a superiority over others and with this a sort of inward elation, this is called pride (takabbur). A proud man will not tolerate any other to be on equal terms with himself. Pride is of three kinds: pride against God, pride against the prophets and saints, and pride against fellow-men. The first is the gravest, for it means setting oneself up in defiance of the Creator; the last is the most common, and consists in despising others and refusing to accept truth from them out of a sense of one's own superiority.
