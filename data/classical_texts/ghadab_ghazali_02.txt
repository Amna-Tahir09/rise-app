SOURCE: Al-Ghazali, Kimiya-yi Sa'adat (The Alchemy of Happiness), trans. Claud Field, 1910, public domain
Some say, "The Law tells us to abstain from anger, lust, and hypocrisy. This is plainly impossible, for man is created with these qualities inherent in him." These foolish people ignore the fact that the Law does not tell us to uproot these passions, but to restrain them within due limits. Even the Prophet said, "I am a man like you, and get angry like others"; and in the Qur'an it is written, "Allah loves those who swallow down their anger," not those who have no anger at all.
