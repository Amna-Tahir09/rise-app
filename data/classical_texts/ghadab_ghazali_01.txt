SOURCE: Al-Ghazali, Kimiya-yi Sa'adat (The Alchemy of Happiness), trans. Claud Field, 1910, public domain
Reason may be called the vizier, or prime minister, passion the revenue-collector, and anger the police-officer. Under the guise of collecting revenue, passion is continually prone to plunder on its own account, while resentment is always inclined to harshness and extreme severity. Both of these have to be kept in due subordination to the king, that is reason, but not killed or expelled, as they have their own proper functions to fulfil. But if passion and resentment master reason, the ruin of the soul infallibly ensues.
