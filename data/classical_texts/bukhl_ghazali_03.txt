SOURCE: Al-Ghazali, "The Alchemy of Happiness," trans. Henry A. Homes, 1873 (Transactions of the Albany Institute, vol. VIII). Public domain.

"If the heart strive not after its own glory and dignity, but inclines to the cares of the world and sensual pleasures, no creature is more feeble, infirm and contemptible than man. At one time he will be the slave of disappointment and melancholy, at another suffering from disease and misfortune; at one time exposed to hunger and thirst, and at another the slave of avarice or ambition."
