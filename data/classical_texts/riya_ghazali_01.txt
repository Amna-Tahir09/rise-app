SOURCE: Al-Ghazali, Kimiya-yi Sa'adat (The Alchemy of Happiness), trans. Claud Field, 1910, public domain
At the Last Day three questions will be asked respecting every action: "Why did you do this?"; "In what way did you do this?"; and "For what purpose did you do this?" The third tests whether the deed was done simply to please Allah, or to gain the approval of men. If a man understands the bearing of these questions he will be very watchful over the state of his heart, and how he entertains thoughts which are likely to end in action.
