SOURCE: Al-Ghazali, Kimiya-yi Sa'adat (The Alchemy of Happiness), trans. Claud Field, 1910, public domain

Take the case of a man who has sold a slave-girl without knowing how much he was attached to her till she is quite out of his reach; then the love of her wakes up in him with such intensity as to amount to torture, stinging him like a snake. Such is the effect of love of wealth and possessions, which those who have it often suspect not till the thing is taken from them.
