SOURCE: Al-Ghazali, Ihya Ulum al-Din, trans. Syed Nawab Ali, 1921, public domain
A sinner once sat near a devout worshipper, hoping to receive his blessing. The worshipper said, "Touch me not, thou filthy sinner, and leave my presence." God then revealed to the prophet of that age: "Go and tell that sinner: thou art forgiven. As for that worshipper, his devotion is cast aside, and he is doomed." The worshipper's outward piety had bred in him a contempt for others that undid the value of his devotion, while the sinner's humility before another human being was accounted to him for good.
