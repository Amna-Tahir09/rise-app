SOURCE: Al-Ghazali, Kimiya-yi Sa'adat (The Alchemy of Happiness), trans. Claud Field, 1910, public domain
The occupation of animals is eating, sleeping, and fighting. Angels contemplate the beauty of Allah and are entirely free from animal qualities. Man is composed of both natures, and must find out which attributes are essential to him and which merely accidental, that he may be delivered from the thraldom of lust and anger rather than ruled by it.
