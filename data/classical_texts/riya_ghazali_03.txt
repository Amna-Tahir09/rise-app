SOURCE: Al-Ghazali, Kimiya-yi Sa'adat (The Alchemy of Happiness), trans. Claud Field, 1910, public domain
Bodily contortions and tearing of clothes sometimes accompany states of ecstasy. If these are the result of genuine ecstatic conditions there is nothing to be said against them, but if they are self-conscious and deliberate on the part of those who wish to appear "adepts," then they are merely acts of hypocrisy. The more perfect one is he who controls himself till he is absolutely obliged to give vent to his feelings.
