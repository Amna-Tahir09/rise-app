SOURCE: Al-Ghazali, Ihya Ulum al-Din, trans. Syed Nawab Ali, 1921, public domain
The consciousness of superiority which begets pride is due to certain attributes which can be summed up under two heads: spiritual attributes, namely knowledge and devotion, and worldly attributes, namely pedigree, beauty, strength, wealth, and following. Of these, pride in knowledge and devotion is the most dangerous, since these are goods in themselves, praised by God and man alike, and it is easy for a person to mistake the elevation they bring for a mark of genuine merit rather than a trial.
