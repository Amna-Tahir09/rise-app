SOURCE: Al-Ghazali, Kimiya-yi Sa'adat (The Alchemy of Happiness), trans. Claud Field, 1910, public domain
The proper nourishment of the soul is the knowledge and love of Allah, and to be absorbed in the love of anything but Allah, including wealth, is the ruin of the soul. The body is simply the riding-animal of the soul; but if a man spends his whole time in feeding and adorning that animal — that is, in accumulating for its sake — the caravan will leave him behind, and he will perish in the desert.
